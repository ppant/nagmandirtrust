ABOUT US

Nag Mandir Trust

Nag Mandir Trust is a community-oriented initiative based in Berinag, Uttarakhand. The Trust envisions spiritual and community centres as places that can also contribute to positive social change.

Through community participation, youth-focused initiatives, fitness activities and cultural engagement, the Trust aims to encourage a healthier, more positive and connected community.
